Privacy Policy

The short version: we don't ask for your personal information, and we use standard advertising analytics.

What we collect

Hair Reviewed does not have accounts, forms, or checkout — we never ask for your name, email, or payment details. Like most websites, we use standard analytics and advertising tools (including the Meta Pixel) that set cookies and collect usage data such as pages visited, device type, and approximate location, to measure our content and advertising.
