How We Review

Our method is boring on purpose. We don't do vibes, unboxing poetry, or "game-changer" — we compare what brands put in writing.

The method

Every comparison on this site is built from four kinds of checkable facts:

  • Spec sheets — weight, materials, wattage, modes, as published by each brand.
  • Listed prices — from each brand's own site, with the month we checked.
  • Written guarantees — the warranty and return terms a brand actually commits to on paper.
  • Verified customer reviews — quoted as written, from real buyers.

If a claim can't be checked against one of those, we don't rank on it. Marketing language is free; labels are commitments.

Who's behind this site

Hair Reviewed is owned and operated by Pleume Paris, the Los Angeles brand behind the hands-free hair dryer — which means our coverage features Pleume products, and you should read it knowing that. We keep this page and the disclosure in every footer because we'd rather you weigh our comparisons with the ownership in view than discover it later. The specs, prices and guarantees we cite are checkable on every brand's own site; we encourage you to check them.
